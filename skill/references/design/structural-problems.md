# Structural Problems to Fix Before Running the Factory

Derived from the original weekly cadence, the cadence is gone (see `factory.md`), but the problems it exposed remain.

## 1. Analysis-heavy, validation-starved

Research and extraction dominate the front of the loop; MVP-building comes before any prospect has confirmed the wedge exists. Building MVPs against un-validated wedges is busywork, not learning.

**Fix:** move validation **before** the MVP. Stage 4 in `factory.md` ("Validate") runs before Stage 5 ("Build"). A wedge with no prospect signal never reaches the MVP stage.

## 2. Outreach against a days-old idea

Cold outreach on a freshly-chosen wedge is directional at best. Reply rate is the only honest signal in that window.

**Fix:** track **reply rate as the single honesty metric**. If it stays <5% across a sustained window, the wedge-**selection** process is broken, not the outreach copy. Do not iterate on outreach; iterate on `personalisation-and-founder-history.md`.

## 3. MVP-as-product vs MVP-as-survey

"The MVP each cycle" implies throwaway code. Fine, but if the MVPs are uninstrumented, the 50 of them produce no data to feed back into analysis.

**Fix:** every MVP is **instrumented from line one**: landing-page analytics, waitlist with source attribution, ≥2 pricing variants (or a pricing-vs-free test). The MVP is a survey of demand, not a product; a survey with no answer sheet is useless.

## What these three share

All three are the same mistake in different clothes: **running the pipeline faster than the signal it produces.** The factory's value is the learning that returns to the SID and Problem Graph, not the artifacts shipped out. If a stage's output cannot become input to the next analysis pass, delete the stage.
